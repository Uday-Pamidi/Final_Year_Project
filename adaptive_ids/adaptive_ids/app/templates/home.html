The DICI Framework autonomously updates its booster trees by correlating real-time network telemetry with dynamic Cyber Threat Intelligence (CTI) feeds, Isolation Forest outlier screening, Kolmogorov-Smirnov drift detection, and SHAP explainability.

System Architecture

Closed-Loop Adaptive Pipeline

How network telemetry flows through hybrid machine learning, CTI correlation, statistical drift surveillance, and explainable AI attribution.

01

Telemetry Ingestion

Extracts 16 statistical flow features: packet velocity, payload entropy, IAT, and TCP flags in line with CIC-IDS standards.

02

Hybrid Classification

Supervised XGBoost identifies known attack classes; unsupervised Isolation Forest flags spatial outliers.

03

Uncertainty Trigger

When confidence falls below threshold (\(\tau = 0.70\)) or an anomaly is detected, IoCs are automatically extracted.

04

CTI Query & Transfer

IoCs are queried against AlienVault OTX, AbuseIPDB, MISP; the CTI Transfer Model correlates intelligence into ground truth.

05

Concept Drift Tests

Two-Sample Kolmogorov-Smirnov, PSI, and ADWIN monitor sliding distribution divergence.

06

Online Retraining

Booster trees are updated incrementally using CTI validated samples and rehearsal replay memory to prevent catastrophic forgetting.

07

Explainable AI (SHAP) Attribution

TreeExplainer generates local feature waterfall attributions and natural-language narratives so SOC analysts understand why an alert fired.

Enterprise Pillars

Engineered for Production SOC Operations

Built to exceed the accuracy, operational agility, and transparency requirements of enterprise security teams.

Hybrid Detection

Combines supervised gradient boosting for known threat classifications with unsupervised anomaly screening for zero-day outliers.

CTI Transfer Model

Transforms external threat intelligence into certified ground truth training vectors, eliminating manual security labeling delays.

Threat Coverage

Multi-Class Network Attack Taxonomy

Trained and benchmarked on CIC-IDS2017 flow distributions with dynamic zero-day mutation handling.

BASELINE

Benign Traffic

Legitimate enterprise HTTP/HTTPS, DNS, SSH sessions exhibiting standard packet sizes and normal flow durations.

DENIAL OF SERVICE

DoS SYN Flood

High packet rates, abnormal forward-to-backward packet ratios, asymmetric traffic volume, and missing ACKs.

RECONNAISSANCE

PortScan

Fast probe sweeps across sequential destination ports, short flow durations, and RST/SYN flag anomalies.

ZERO-DAY

Mutated Zero-Day

Unseen distribution vectors with high entropy, flagged as outliers by Isolation Forest and submitted for CTI correlation.
